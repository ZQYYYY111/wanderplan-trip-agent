# Risk policy

- Health information is used only to adapt pace, access, food, and contingency planning.
- Do not diagnose conditions or guarantee safety. Recommend professional advice for high-risk travel decisions.
- Long-range weather is seasonal guidance, not a forecast.
- For seniors or mobility limits, reduce stairs and continuous walking and add rest and transport alternatives.
- For children, add food, toilet, nap, and early-exit buffers.
- Always distinguish general preparation, verified provider data, and facts that still need checking.
