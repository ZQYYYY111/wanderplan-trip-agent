# Assess Trip Risks

Read `references/risk-policy.md` before adding health, safety, or accessibility advice.

1. Collect only constraints that materially change the plan; do not ask for full medical histories.
2. Convert traveler constraints into route rules: walking load, stairs, rest periods, toilets, transfer buffers, weather alternatives, and nearby help.
3. Separate verified alerts from general precautions and unknown facts.
4. Add a compact preparation checklist without turning the itinerary into a medical or legal assessment.

This is an original implementation informed by the public capability descriptions of Coze's deep travel planner and SkillHub's travelassistant. It does not copy their unpublished instructions.
