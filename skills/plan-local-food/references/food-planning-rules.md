# Food planning rules

- Prefer meal areas within 20–30 minutes of the previous or next activity.
- For each main meal, state area, two to four representative dishes or restaurant types, per-person range, and why it fits the route.
- Keep at least one flexible meal when arrival time is uncertain.
- Repeat allergy, vegetarian, halal, or spice constraints where a meal is suggested.
- Search links help the user verify candidates; they are not evidence of hours, reviews, or availability.
- Never book, reserve, order, or collect payment.
